Use this checklist for every scheduled weekly research pass.

## Discover new people
Search for additional well-known or demonstrably experienced crypto/market traders whose public material contains implementation-relevant methods. Do not add someone merely for subscriber count.

Candidate priority:
- independently auditable/professional history;
- long-form educational/live-trading material;
- explicit risk/process information;
- transparent failures/drawdowns;
- data/platform footprint that can be verified;
- methods applicable or adaptable to Belgium-compliant crypto spot;
- usefulness to a small account after real costs.

## For every person — old and new
Apply `STRATEGY_TRUTH_STANDARD.md` in full.

Research changes in:
- biography/professional status;
- platforms/exchanges/data feeds;
- new videos/courses/interviews;
- new/changed strategies;
- exact setup/entry/stop/sizing/management/exit;
- when/when-not-to-use;
- public live trade walkthroughs;
- performance evidence;
- failures/drawdowns/incorrect calls;
- regulatory/product relevance;
- Kraken/EU compatibility;
- small-account economics.

## Weekly output
Produce:
1. `Verified changes this week`
2. `New traders worth adding`
3. `Strategy rule changes`
4. `New live-trade/video extractions`
5. `New performance/failure evidence`
6. `Belgium/Kraken changes`
7. `Handoff/catalog changes required`
8. `Unverified claims rejected`
9. `Sources`

If nothing verifiable changed, explicitly say so. Never manufacture an update to make the report look active.
